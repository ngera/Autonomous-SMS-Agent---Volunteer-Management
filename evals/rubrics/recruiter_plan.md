# Recruiter Wave Plan Rubric

Score the recruiter agent's wave plan on each axis from 1 (broken) to 5
(excellent). All four axes scored independently.

## Axes

### right_sized_first_wave

Is the first wave neither over- nor under-targeting?

Reference: first wave should target ~2x the smallest service's min, or
up to half the available pool, whichever is smaller.

- **5** — Hits the reference window precisely.
- **4** — Within 20% of the reference window.
- **3** — 21-40% off.
- **2** — More than 40% off in either direction.
- **1** — First wave of zero, OR targets entire pool at once.

### service_volunteer_fit

Did the plan pick volunteers who have done this service before, where
available?

- **5** — Every assignment maps to a volunteer with prior history in
  that service (when history exists).
- **4** — Most assignments use prior-history volunteers.
- **3** — Mixed — uses history where convenient.
- **2** — Ignores history; picks alphabetically or randomly.
- **1** — Actively picks volunteers known to have refused this service
  before.

### honors_privacy

Does the plan respect `share_on_roster` = hidden volunteers?

- **5** — Hidden volunteers are eligible for picking but NEVER named in
  any explanatory text exposed to other volunteers.
- **4** — Hidden volunteers used; no exposure issues.
- **3** — Plan names hidden volunteers in internal admin-facing text only.
- **2** — Plan exposes hidden volunteer names in volunteer-facing text.
- **1** — Plan publishes hidden volunteer phone numbers.

### message_voice

Is the wave SMS draft warm, brief, gives an out, no marketing speak?

- **5** — All wave messages feel like a real coordinator wrote them.
- **4** — Pleasant; minor stiffness or formality.
- **3** — Functional but bland.
- **2** — Marketing speak in some messages.
- **1** — Demanding, pushy, or off-puttingly enthusiastic.
